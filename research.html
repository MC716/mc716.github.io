Research

How molecular motion shapes chemical selectivity.

I work across molecular dynamics, electronic structure theory, and mechanistic modeling, with an emphasis on catalytic systems whose behavior cannot be captured by a single static structure.

Dynamics-informed asymmetric catalysis

Using classical and QM/MM molecular dynamics to understand flexibility, induced fit, solvent response, and stereocontrol in chiral dirhodium catalysts.

  • Dirhodium tetracarboxylates and tetraprolinates
  • Carbene C–H insertion and cyclopropanation
  • Noncovalent interactions and facial selectivity

Predictive models of enantioinduction

Connecting conformational ensembles and energetics to quantitative predictions of reaction selectivity.

  • MM/GBSA and ensemble-based energetic descriptors
  • Mechanism-informed selectivity models
  • Machine learning for pattern recognition and catalyst classification

QM/MM and enhanced sampling

Exploring reaction-relevant conformational space in explicit solvent using multiscale dynamics and biasing methods.

  • QM/MM molecular dynamics
  • Metadynamics and constrained simulations
  • Free-energy landscapes and collective variables

Broader mechanistic chemistry

Applying computational chemistry to transition-metal catalysis, carbene philicity, solid-state reactivity, interfacial chemistry, and CO₂ capture.

  • DFT and electronic-structure analysis
  • Topochemical and solid-state reaction dynamics
  • Silica surfaces and aminosilane behavior
