About

Computational chemistry across scales

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Chemistry at the University of Pittsburgh in the group of Prof. Peng Liu. Originally from Kerala, India, I completed an integrated BS–MS degree at IISER Thiruvananthapuram before joining Pitt in 2022. My work focuses broadly on computational elucidation of mechanistic features in homogeneous catalysis, with particular emphasis on chiral dirhodium catalysis, molecular dynamics, noncovalent interactions, and dynamics-informed models of stereoselectivity. My broader interests include transition-metal catalysis, reaction dynamics, CO₂ capture, solid-state reactivity, and theoretical studies of noncovalent interactions.
