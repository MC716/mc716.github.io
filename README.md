# Mithun C. Madhusudhanan

Computational and theoretical chemist working at the interface of molecular dynamics,
quantum chemistry, mechanistic analysis, and catalyst design.

## Profile

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Chemistry at the University of Pittsburgh working with
Prof. Peng Liu. I joined the University of Pittsburgh in 2022 after completing an
integrated BS–MS degree at IISER Thiruvananthapuram.

My research focuses broadly on computational mechanistic chemistry, with a primary
emphasis on understanding chiral dirhodium catalysis through molecular dynamics.
I am particularly interested in how catalyst flexibility, conformational dynamics,
solvent effects, and noncovalent interactions govern stereoselectivity.

My work combines classical molecular dynamics, QM/MM simulations, enhanced sampling,
density functional theory, free-energy analysis, and data-driven modeling to build
dynamics-informed descriptions of catalytic reactivity and enantioinduction.

Broader research interests include:

- Transition-metal catalysis
- Chiral dirhodium chemistry
- Carbene reactivity and C–H insertion
- Molecular dynamics and reaction dynamics
- QM/MM and enhanced sampling
- Noncovalent interactions
- Solid-state and topochemical reactivity
- CO₂ capture and interfacial chemistry
- Mechanistic machine learning and descriptor development
